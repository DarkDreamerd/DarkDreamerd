### What I do at Echo

I build the Echo staff. I decide who joins the team, who moves up and who leaves, and I keep the staff rulebook together with the Project Lead.

I lead four departments: Marketing & Partnerships, Customer Support, Quality Assurance and Trust & Safety. Each one has a head and curators, and they report to me.

I represent Echo outside the team: I talk to partners and other projects and stand up for the project's interests.

Appeals against moderation decisions and ad requests for the [server catalog](https://monitoring.echo-assistants.tech) also come to me.

I play on Echo · Minecraft too.
